Interactive Microeconomics

This site provides interactive materials that follow the lecture notes for introductory microeconomics. Each page uses sliders, graphs, and simple calculations to help students explore the main ideas visually.

Created by Shigeki Isogai, School of Economics and Management, University of Hyogo.
