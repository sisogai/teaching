6.1 Partial versus General Equilibrium

Partial equilibrium holds other markets fixed. General equilibrium tracks how markets interact. With mobile labor, for example, a tax in one town may affect wages elsewhere and shift the burden in a way partial analysis misses.

6.2 Pure Exchange Economy

In a two-good, two-consumer exchange economy, an allocation can be represented in an Edgeworth box. A Walrasian equilibrium is a price vector such that the two consumers' demands are mutually feasible.

Welfare theorems

Under standard conditions, Walrasian equilibrium allocations are Pareto efficient. Conversely, a desired Pareto-efficient allocation can be decentralized by suitable redistribution of initial endowments.
