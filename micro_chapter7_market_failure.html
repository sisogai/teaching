7.1 Externality

An externality occurs when an economic activity affects other agents' welfare without passing through the market mechanism. The key distinction is between private marginal cost and social marginal cost.

7.2 Public Goods

Public goods are non-rival and non-excludable. Efficient provision vertically sums individual marginal values and compares that social marginal value with marginal cost.
