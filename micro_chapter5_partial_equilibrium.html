5.1 Perfect Competition and Policy

Partial equilibrium analysis focuses on one market. In a perfectly competitive benchmark, equilibrium is where demand intersects marginal cost, and total surplus is maximized under the maintained assumptions.

Commodity taxes and binding price regulations move the market away from the efficient quantity and create deadweight loss.
